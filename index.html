Privacy Policy for Decision 2.0

Effective Date: November 6, 2025

Last Updated: November 6, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Decision 2.0 is designed to help you make decisions locally on your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy and do not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Data We DO NOT Collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Contacts, photos, or other device content
  • Decision history or app usage patterns

Data That May Be Stored Locally:

  • App preferences and settings (stored only on your device)
  • Decision history (if enabled, stored locally only)

2. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, we do not use, share, or process any personal data. All app functionality operates entirely on your device.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any personal information to third parties because we do not collect such information.

4. Data Security

Any local data stored by the app remains on your device and is subject to your device's security measures. We recommend keeping your device secure with appropriate lock screens and security settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. The app is safe for users of all ages.

6. Third-Party Services

Decision 2.0 does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or social media services that would collect your data.

7. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions for functionality purposes only:

  • Internet Access: Not currently used, reserved for future updates
  • Vibration: For haptic feedback during decision-making
  • Storage: To save app preferences locally on your device

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. You can clear all local app data by uninstalling and reinstalling the app.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
